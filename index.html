A Quiet Ascension

Where Light Rests

Stillness has a shape. Scroll to find it.

Grace does not arrive loudly.
It settles, like dust in a sunbeam,
already home.

I

Every falling feather
knew, once, how to fly —
and chose to rest instead.

II

What is holy is not distant.
It is only quiet
enough to hear.

III

Concentric, patient, unhurried — the way light moves when no one is watching.

Amen, Softly

Carry this stillness back down with you.
